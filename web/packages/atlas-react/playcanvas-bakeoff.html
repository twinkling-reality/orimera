starting
Click the canvas to take pointer lock, then WASD to move and the mouse to look. Escape releases the mouse and is never bound by this page. Targeting is the reticle at screen centre, because pointer lock freezes the cursor coordinates. Parameters: ?points=1000000&islands=1&device=webgl2&measure=15. Results are printed to the console, one line each, prefixed ORIMERA-BAKEOFF.
